# Candidate B variations: every palette colour, tint, outline and marker strength

Follow-up to `2026-11-06-highlighter-survey` after Patrick picked candidate B
(faint ink-preserving tint plus a marker-coloured outline) and asked for more
colours and variations. Pillow sketches with the app's background estimate and
coverage unmix; text pixels are untouched in every tile.

Dark pages (`b-variations-dark-table.png`, `b-variations-dark-notice.png`),
eight variations × seven palette colours:
V1 2 px outline + faint tint (+0.05 luminance); V2 outline, no tint; V3 3 px
outline + tint +0.08; V4 rounded outline; V5 1 px outline inside the box +
tint +0.08; V6 outline at 70 % opacity; V7 outline + underline per text line;
V8 tint +0.10 only, no outline.

Light pages (`b-variations-light.png`), six variations: marker lifted to paper
luminance 0.45–0.7 (what the app does now) with and without the outline,
pastel 0.6–0.8 with and without, strong 0.35–0.65, very pale 0.75–0.9 with
the outline.
