## Acceptance checklist: GNOME, Hyprland, Budgie (labwc)

From the CI packages of this PR's head (GNOME: the Fedora 44 RPM on f44 or the Ubuntu GNOME guest; Hyprland: the Arch package or the AppImage on the Omarchy guest; Budgie: the AppImage on the Fedora 45 Budgie guest). Under the desktop lock, with `PUTGD_RECORD_DEBUG_LOG` set for the resident's helper, Firefox opened by the run at a known page (the user guide, Wikipedia "Linux", Stack Overflow 11227809), local save only.

1. **You scroll, nothing injected.** `putgd-cli scroll.prepare '{"rect":[…],"driver":"user"}'`, then `scroll.start`. Turn the wheel with a relative mouse from `/dev/uinput` (the lab's `umouse.py` with `w N`) at a person's pace to the end, once back up, then `scroll.stop`. Pass: the helper log has no `scroll inject:` and no `scroll step:` line; the result is `endReason: done`, `truncated: false`, `scrolledBy: user`; the PNG equals Firefox's own full-page picture in 64-row bands outside what the page pins (`tools/lab/scroll_tracker_score.py` comparison); a sticky header or side bar is in it once.
2. **Frame rate.** The log line `scroll: source N frames in M ms (X per second)` of run 1: 20 or more a second (GNOME: Mutter's area stream with the 40 ms beat; Hyprland and Budgie: `ext-image-copy-capture` on damage). Report the number.
3. **Auto-scroll from the bar.** Start with `driver: user`, keep the mouse still, click **Auto-scroll** (it begins after 500 ms of stillness). Pass: it runs to the end by itself, `end_of_content` (or `done` with `reachedEnd: true` and `framesSkipped` above 0), the whole page. Hyprland: no painted arrow anywhere in the image.
4. **The mouse pauses it.** During auto-scroll move the relative mouse toward the bar at about 600 px/s. Pass: progress `state: paused`, `pausedBy: pointer`, `driver: user` within tens of ms; no `scroll inject:` line after the first motion (on Wayland at most one `pointer put back`); the bar says "Paused: you moved the mouse"; the watch is gone (`scroll: watch over` once per auto-scroll run, none while paused). Scroll a little yourself, then **Auto-scroll** again: one exact image, `scrolledBy: both`.
5. **Done and Cancel in every state** (waiting, following, lost after a scrollbar jump, auto-scrolling, paused): Done keeps an exact image (`lost_track` when lost), Cancel leaves no History row and no staging files.
6. **The key.** Bind a `scroll` hotkey; the bar names it ("Done or <key> when you reach the end"); pressing it during a capture keeps the image. Without a binding the bar says "Done when you reach the end".
7. **A region that fills the output.** The bar sits inside the region's bottom-right corner (GNOME: the Shell's inside placement, the resident sends `overlay {visible: true, rect, inside: true}`; log `scroll: no room beside the rectangle` on Hyprland and Budgie). Done and Cancel work; no bar pixel in the image; the page's last rows under the bar missing at the very end is expected.
8. **Nothing takes input over the region while you scroll.** GNOME: the ring and the beat are Shell actors that are not reactive; the extension stays at version 23 (no logout needed). Hyprland and Budgie: the ring's layer surfaces pass the wheel and clicks. Check that a click on a link inside the region during a "you scroll" capture opens it.
9. **Agent control.** The same through MCP: `prepare_scroll_capture` with `driver`, `control_scroll_capture` `auto_scroll` on/off, `get_scroll_capture` showing `activity`, `driver`, `placed`, `skipped`.
