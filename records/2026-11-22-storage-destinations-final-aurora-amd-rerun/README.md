## Restoration

The two cancelled uploads and the linked capture were absent from SFTP. I removed the test destination, restored local capture output, closed the scoped resident and synthetic display, and removed the private CA bundle. The Firefox handler and SSH client configuration were restored byte for byte. The disposable Firefox profile and its imported CA were removed. The original state of the put.gd RPM and the system trust bundle checksum matched the baseline, and the desktop lease was released at 15:13:56 UTC after cleanup verification. The synthetic display did not exit after SIGTERM or its quit command, so I terminated its verified PID and repeated the cleanup check.
